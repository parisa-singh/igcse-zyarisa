The toolkit

Every resource, with a reason.

There's an ocean of IGCSE resources online, and most of it wastes your time. This is the short list that doesn't — official documents first, then the sites and channels I'd actually send you to, each with a note on when to use it.

Start here — these are free and official

Cambridge official resources

Third-party sites paraphrase these. Go to the source. All free, all on cambridgeinternational.org.

1. Syllabus most important

The single most important document you own. Contains every topic, the command-words glossary, the assessment objectives, and the traffic-light topic checklist. Download it for every subject before term starts and never invent your own revision list again.

Find it: cambridgeinternational.org → Find a syllabus → IGCSE → [subject] → Syllabus PDF

2. Specimen papers + mark schemes

Released whenever a syllabus changes. They show the current paper format and mark-scheme structure. Do these before touching older past papers so you know what today's exam actually looks like.

Find it: same subject page, "Specimen papers" section.

3. Learner Guide

Cambridge's plain-English summary of the syllabus, written for students rather than teachers. Not every subject has one — check. Where it exists, it's the gentlest way in.

Find it: subject page → "Support materials".

4. Principal Examiner's Report

Published after every exam series. The examiner writes, in plain words, exactly what students got wrong, what language they wanted, and where marks were lost. Almost nobody reads these. Read the report for every past paper you attempt.

Find it: on the past-paper archives below, filed alongside each session's papers.

5. Grade threshold tables

The minimum marks for each grade (A*, A, B…) per paper and session. Use them to calibrate: how many marks do you actually need? Often lower than you'd fear — which changes how you triage questions.

Find it: cambridgeinternational.org → exam administration → results → grade thresholds.

6. Cambridge GO

The official digital platform for Cambridge-endorsed textbooks. If your school uses Cambridge coursebooks, the digital versions may be accessible here with a code from school. Ask your teacher.

Find it: cambridge.org/go

YouTube worth your time

One sentence on each — what it covers and why it's worth watching. For anything not listed, the reliable search pattern is "IGCSE [subject code] [topic]".

General IGCSE

  • Search "IGCSE [subject] [topic]" — the strongest channels surface this way.
  • The r/IGCSE wiki has a student-maintained resource list per subject.

Sciences (Physics)

  • Cognito — clear, animated explanations mapped to IGCSE specs.
  • Science Shorts — past-paper worked solutions.

Computer Science

  • Craig'n'Dave — the single best channel for IGCSE CS, past-paper walkthroughs included.
  • Search "IGCSE 0478 [topic]" for pseudocode specifics.

Business + Economics

  • IGCSE Business Revision — structured topic videos.
  • Search "IGCSE Economics 0455 [topic]" for targeted content.

Maths (0607)

  • The Organic Chemistry Tutor — topic explanations with calculator use shown.
  • Casio Education (official) — calculator operation tutorials.

English Language + Literature

  • Snap Revise — structured analysis techniques.
  • Mr Salles Teaches English — marking and annotation approach.
