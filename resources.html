The toolkit

Every resource, with a reason.

There's an ocean of IGCSE resources online and most of it wastes your time. This is the short list that doesn't — official docs first, then the archives, platforms, and channels I'd actually send you to. Every one is labelled so you know what's free before you click.

Text-analysis sites — English Literature (0475)

Read this first

These are built for general literature study, not Cambridge 0475. Use them to understand texts, themes, and characters — never for mark-scheme technique, and never as a substitute for reading the text. Always cross-check scope against the syllabus.

ResourceAccessNotes
SparkNotesFreeFree with ads. Chapter summaries, character and theme overviews for most set texts. Quick plot/theme check.
LitChartsFreemiumBy the original SparkNotes editors. Colour-coded theme trackers, device glossary, Shakespeare with modern translation. Free online; PDFs/quote explanations paid (~$10/mo). Best-designed of the three.
GradeSaverFreemiumFree chapter summaries, quizzes, theme guides; full guides paid. Real student essays show what a structured argument looks like.
